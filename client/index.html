        About

        Bandits is a free-for-all dogfight. Every pilot starts as a Cadet. Shoot planes down and fly through the salvage they leave, and your plane grows: more hull, more guns, harder hits. It also turns slower, so a small plane can always out-turn a big one.

        When the sky is quiet, a few bots (marked bot) keep it busy. They make way as people arrive.

        What we keep

        No accounts. Your call sign and, if you leave it on, your country flag are shown to the other pilots. Scores over 30 go on today’s board with that name and flag, and are deleted after three days. Your IP address is never stored.
